中文

纯属娱乐与模型研究，不用于投资决策。免费，无返佣。

方向日历与所谓收益均为固定时间模型的假设统计，不是实际交易、投资建议、未来价格预测或盈利保证。模型有效性未经证明；费用、样本重叠、缺口和未完成段会影响统计，资金费与杠杆风险未被模拟。

数据连接面向符合所在地规定和 OKX 条件的中国大陆以外使用者；中国大陆与其他受限地区不可使用。页面的地区与用途选项只是自声明，不是 IP 封锁、法律认证或 OKX 授权。必须由用户本人判断资格并主动操作；连接可达不证明获得使用许可。

官方数据条件

OKX API 协议第 9.3、9.4 节限制向第三方提供 API 服务及行情再分发、展示；免密公开端点同样适用。未经所需书面许可，不应公开分享行情或带真实行情的截图。另请阅读 OKX 服务条款。

免费与娱乐标签不豁免适用法律或 OKX 数据条件。项目没有宣称获得 OKX 再分发授权，浏览器直连也不构成此类授权。条件不明或受到限制时应停止，不用代理规避。

默认不请求行情、不展示方向；主动连接成功后显示图表和模型日历，回测须另点“运行回测”。连接只读 GET、内存处理，刷新或“取消 / 清除本次数据”即丢弃，不下单、不代理转发、不持续自动刷新。不可改选官方服务区域规避限制。文档合成结果图仅为说明界面的演示，不是授权依据或真实市场结果。

本说明针对当前应用与包，不表示旧提交、旧截图或旧发布包已删除。

English

For entertainment and model research only. Do not use it for investment decisions. Free, without referral commissions.

Directions and returns are hypothetical outputs of a fixed time model, not executions, advice, price forecasts, or profit guarantees. Model validity is unproven. Costs, overlapping samples, gaps, and unfinished segments affect the statistics; funding and leverage risks are not simulated.

Connection is for independently eligible users outside mainland China and other restricted locations. Region and purpose options are self-declarations, not IP blocking, legal certification, or OKX approval. Users must determine eligibility and actively connect themselves. Technical access establishes no permission.

Official data conditions

Sections 9.3 and 9.4 of the OKX API Agreement restrict third-party API provision and market-data redistribution or display, including unauthenticated public endpoints. Do not publish prices or real-price screenshots without required written permission. Read the OKX Terms of Service as well.

Free or entertainment use waives no applicable obligations. The project claims no OKX redistribution approval; direct browser access does not confer it. Stop if restricted or uncertain, without a circumvention proxy.

Startup makes no market-data request or direction display. Successful loading reveals charts and the model calendar; statistics require a separate “运行回测” action. Read-only GET data remain in page memory, discarded on refresh or “取消 / 清除本次数据”, without orders, forwarding, or continuous refresh. Do not switch service regions to bypass restrictions. Synthetic documentation results merely illustrate the interface.
